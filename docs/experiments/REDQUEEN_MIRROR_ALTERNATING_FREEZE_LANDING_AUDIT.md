## 3. Mirror alternating-freeze meaning

Mirror alternating-freeze means there are two lanes or roles. One lane is active and may receive RedQueen-directed sample pressure, review pressure, or scheduling pressure. The other lane is frozen and must remain immutable during that phase. The frozen mirror can evaluate, compare, anchor, or provide disagreement feedback. Phases alternate, lane state is recorded, illegal updates to the frozen lane are rejected, simultaneous active lanes are rejected, default profile cannot reach this path, and explicit opt-in remains required.

## 4. Co-symbiosis meaning

Co-symbiosis means active lane and frozen mirror lane exchange metrics. Mirror disagreement or stability deltas feed RedQueen governance. RedQueen may adjust sampling, difficulty, or review pressure. The mirror cannot bypass AtomicSynthesis, ExtendedIR, or ExtendedEmitterC. The mirror cannot enable production promotion or mutate the default profile.
